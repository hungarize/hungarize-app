Privacy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: the Hungarize app for Mac keeps your study data on your own Mac. We don't run ads, we don't track you, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your notes, starred words, flashcard progress, listening and exercise statistics, and your settings (for example your name and birthday, which the app uses for greetings).
  • Your Patreon sign-in. You sign in on Patreon's own page inside the app; Patreon keeps the sign-in in the app's local web storage, just as a browser would. We never see your password.
  • Downloaded podcast transcripts, so they also open offline.

What the app reads from the internet

  • Patreon: the Hungarize posts you open, your membership tier (to show the study tools that come with it) and, for posts you can view, their audio and attachments. This is the same as using Patreon in a browser and is covered by Patreon's privacy policy.
  • GitHub: app updates and the Home screen's daily content, from our public hungarize-app project.
  • Notion: our public dialogue database, shown in the app's Database tab.
  • YouTube: our own videos, played in YouTube's embedded player, under YouTube's terms and Google's privacy policy.

What you can choose to send us

If you use Send Feedback, what you write (and a screenshot, if you add one) goes to our feedback inbox, so we can read it and reply. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

YouTube subtitles (Hungarize Captions)

To show our videos' Hungarian and English subtitles in the app, an automated service run by Hungarize reads the subtitle files of our own YouTube channel's videos through the official YouTube Data API, using our own Google account. It does not access any student's Google or YouTube account and collects no personal data. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Your study data lives only on your Mac: deleting the app and its folder in ~/Library/Application Support removes it. In the app, Settings also lets you delete your statistics, and signing out of Patreon removes your sign-in.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us through the app's Send Feedback form or message us on our Patreon page.
