Install Hungarize on your Mac

Thank you for testing Hungarize! Installing takes about two minutes. Follow the five steps below, and you'll be studying in no time.

Download Hungarize

Free for beta testers · about 8 MB · for Macs with macOS 13 Ventura or newer

You only do this once

After the first time, Hungarize opens like any other app. New versions install themselves, so you never have to repeat these steps.

Why macOS asks first

Hungarize is still in beta, so Apple hasn't reviewed it yet. Until then, macOS asks you to confirm that you trust it. That's the extra security step, and it's completely normal.

Step by step

The pictures show what you'll see on your Mac. Click the orange-circled button in each one.

  1. 1

    Download and open the file

    Click Download Hungarize at the top of this page. When it's done, open Hungarize.dmg from your Downloads folder (double-click it). A small Hungarize window opens.

    Downloads
  2. 2

    Drag Hungarize into Applications

    In that window, drag the Hungarize icon onto the Applications folder, just like the arrow shows. That copies the app to your Mac.

  3. 3

    Open Hungarize, then click Done

    Open your Applications folder and double-click Hungarize. The first time, macOS says it couldn't check the app. That's expected for a beta app: click Done and go on to step 4.

    Don't click Move to Bin. Nothing is wrong with the app, macOS just needs your OK first.

    ⚠️
    “Hungarize” Not Opened Apple could not verify “Hungarize” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.
    Done Move to Bin
  4. 4

    Allow it in System Settings

    Open System Settings (click the Apple logo in the top-left corner of your screen), click Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. Next to “Hungarize was blocked”, click Open Anyway, then enter your Mac's password (or use Touch ID).

    Shortcut: the Hungarize window from step 1 has an Open Settings icon. Double-click it to go straight there.

    System Settings › Privacy & Security Security
    “Hungarize” was blocked to protect your Mac. Open Anyway
    Password ••••••••
  5. 5

    Confirm one last time

    macOS asks once more whether to open Hungarize. Click Open Anyway. That's it! Hungarize opens, and from now on it starts like any other app.

    Open “Hungarize”?

You're in. What happens next?

  • Log in with your Patreon account. Hungarize opens Patreon's login page first, then a short welcome sets the app up for you.
  • Tidy up if you like. You can delete Hungarize.dmg from your Downloads folder. The app stays in Applications.
  • Updates come by themselves. When a new beta version is ready, Hungarize offers to install it. One click, and you're up to date.
  • Tell us what you think. Use the Feedback button at the bottom of Hungarize's Home screen. Every note helps us make the app better.
